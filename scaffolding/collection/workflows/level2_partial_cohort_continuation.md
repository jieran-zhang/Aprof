# Explicit continuation of the interrupted Dynamic Quant search

`level2_cohort_continuation.py` handles one recorded interruption of
`dynamic_quant001-search`. It is a bounded recovery of the original charged
timing attempt, not a general retry policy. The original source, parent,
six-process design, repeat settings and search cases remain frozen.

The first two successful processes are reused. The failed third process and its
scratch capture are preserved with exact inventories and hashes. A separately
named third process supplies the missing formal slot; the remaining three
processes keep their original planned names. Original missing profiler and
owner completion receipts stay missing. The known native and owner exits do
not establish the unknown profiler exit.

Authorization requires externally bound terminal, preservation and actual new
engineering records. Before creating the one-shot continuation namespace, the
tool validates the prospective certificate, event prefixes, source, producer
CAS and current engineering. Execution checks these bindings again and verifies
the preserved TAR contents at admission and before publishing a successful
report. Historical Python snapshots are evidence only and are never executed.

Successful completion contains six accepted processes and 4,320 formal role
samples. Actual costs include the original failed process: seven process
starts, six successful processes, 4,680 role samples, 46,800 timed complete
calls and 1,560 warmup complete calls. Kernel launch counts come from journals;
a complete call is not assumed to contain one kernel. The continuation's wall
duration excludes the earlier collection and interruption gap, which are
explicitly unaccounted for rather than zero.

The ledger records `qualify_continuation` without another source attempt,
round, stage start or timing-group charge. After actual successful completion,
the ordinary candidate coordinator may close feedback with output
deduplication disabled. Another failure requires inspection; neither this
module nor the old owner automatically retries it.

Report, feedback and archive readers authenticate the same physical mapping
and preserve failed costs and interruption limitations. Performance qualification
stays false. This research evidence does not establish formal runtime admission
or eligibility for a training graph episode. Output retirement and deduplication
remain unsupported for this continued cohort.

Ordinary historical cohorts do not load the continuation or preservation
modules. Offline packaging of a continued cohort must explicitly include both
modules and the certificate's complete evidence closure. Metadata-only replay
does not claim that TAR payload bytes were streamed and verified.

The raw evidence seal reader/writer also uses a bounded 64 MiB metadata limit,
checked before publication. A real full-workload seal exceeded the previous
16 MiB limit. This changes neither the existing seal schemas nor historical
failed qualification records.
